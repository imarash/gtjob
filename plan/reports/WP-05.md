# WP-05 report: writing references

## Summary

Wrote three skill reference guides in the section 13 layout (`## Contents`, `## Core`, `## Details` with
Purpose/Inputs/Rules/Output/Self-check, `## Examples`) and a structure/anonymization test.

- Sample-CV examples are anonymized (no name, email, website, client or employer names; real numbers and technologies kept).
