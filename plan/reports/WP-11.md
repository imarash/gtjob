# WP-11 report: attestation store, scope resolution, `attest`

## Summary
- `attestations.py`: `empty_store`, `load_store` (empty in-memory store when the file is missing; nothing written),
  `save_store` (schema-validated, atomic via `jsonio.dump_json`, rejects another person's store), `add` (`att-%03d`
  from `next_seq`, `status: proposed`, timestamps, `run_id`/`question_id`, deep-copies the 9 proposal fields),
  `get`, `set_status` (proposed→active|revoked, active→revoked, else `E_ATTEST_TRANSITION`), `usable`,
  `find_by_terms`.
- `scope.py`: `resolve` (role_label = case-insensitive whole-word match on engagement `role_label` / work `position`;
  `items` validated, deduped, document order, `E_SCOPE_UNKNOWN_ID`; `global`/`unresolved` -> `item_ids: []`),
  `describe_mapping`, `candidates(description, profile, limit=4)` (rank: role-label hits, name hits, keyword
  overlap; stopword-filtered, plural-insensitive), `all_items`, `whole_word_match`.
- `cvt attest list|show|revoke|activate [ID] [RUN] [--person P] [--json]`; the store comes from RUN's `person_slug`,
  or `--person`, or the latest run. `activate` refuses an `unresolved` scope (`E_ATTEST_SCOPE_UNRESOLVED`).
- Fixture `tests/fixtures/profile/nested-engagements.json`: invented person, 3 employers, 10 engagements, SRE at
  `exp-2.e4` (Harborline Bank) and `exp-2.e5`; validated against the profile schema in the test.

- `describe_mapping` labels follow the contract example (`"Harborline Bank — SRE (Fernbrook Consulting)"`), not the
  brief's `"(at Mobia)"` wording. The two disagree; the frozen contract wins.
- The brief names two `candidates` functions. `candidates(description, profile, limit)` ranks;
  `candidates(profile)` (a dict as the only argument) lists everything. `all_items(profile)` is the explicit name.
