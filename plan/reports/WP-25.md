## ESCALATION

1. `briefs.py` (WP-29) / `interfaces.md` 14.4: the `answer_parse` packet on the private sample is 3067 tokens vs the 2500 budget
   (`warning[budget]`). The untrimmable sections are the 8 pending questions plus the full item-header digest (~2,000 tokens),
   for a 90-character reply. Fix: cap the digest in `answer_parse` to the items named by the questions, or raise the budget.
   The e2e flow does not enforce this one packet; the strict xfail flips to a failure once fixed (remove the marker).
2. `cvt questions` (WP-15): suppressing a gap choice leaves its name in the prompt ("... service mesh; ..."), so a pending
   question still *mentions* "service mesh" on the second run (WP-20 feedback 10). Choices are asserted clean; the prompt
   check is the second strict xfail. Fix: rewrite or strip suppressed terms from the prompt in `questions.py`.
